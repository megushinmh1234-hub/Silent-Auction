What Is a Silent Auction?

How a silent auction works and who your bids support

Silent Paper Bidding

A silent auction is an event where people bid on items by writing their offers on paper bidding sheets. There is no need to shout out bids. After bidding closes, the highest valid bidder wins each item according to the event rules.

Our Charity: Second Harvest Nagoya

Second Harvest Nagoya helps provide food to people who need it. Our event will directly support this cause—every bid helps make a real difference in our community.

How to Place Your Bid

Follow these 4 simple steps during the event

1

Browse the Items

Look at the available items and read their descriptions.

2

Choose an Item

Find something you would like to win and locate its bidding sheet.

3

Write Your Bid

Write your name or bidder number and your bid amount clearly.

4

Wait for Results

After bidding closes, the organizers will check the sheets and announce the winners.

Bidding Tip: Check the current highest bid and follow the minimum bidding increase shown on the sheet.

Auction Rules

Please keep the auction fair and enjoyable for everyone

  • Only bid an amount you are willing and able to pay if you win.
  • Follow each item's starting bid and minimum increase.
  • Write your bid clearly and check it before submitting.
  • Never change or interfere with another person's bid.
  • Submit your bid before the official closing time.
  • Be respectful of everyone and all auction items.
  • Ask an organizer or supervising teacher if you need help.
  • The organizers will resolve ties and unclear bids according to the event policy.
Important: Bidding lasts 1 hour and 30 minutes. Stop bidding when the organizers announce that the auction has closed.
